EXTEND AND REFINE THE EXISTING IMMERSIVE JAPAN EXPERIENCE

This project is inspired by Moooi's immersive digital storytelling experiences.

The website should feel like a cinematic journey through Japan rather than a traditional website.

Every transition, animation, interaction, panel, and page should feel like part of a single continuous experience.

The final result should be investor-demo quality and suitable for presenting a future immersive tourism and cultural exploration platform.

---

## OVERALL VISUAL DIRECTION

The entire experience should resemble:

* A luxury digital museum
* A premium cultural exhibition
* A world-class tourism experience
* A cinematic travel documentary
* An architectural visualization
* A high-end editorial experience

Every scene should feel immersive, elegant, calm, and emotionally engaging.

Avoid anything that feels like a typical startup landing page, game UI, or generic website.

---

## VISUAL ASSET QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

The quality of the imagery is one of the most important aspects of this project.

Use only:

* Ultra-high-resolution professional photography
* Museum-quality architectural photography
* Premium landscape photography
* Authentic Japanese heritage site photography
* High-quality photorealistic environment renders when photography is not suitable
* Natural lighting
* Rich environmental detail
* Ultra-sharp textures
* Cinematic composition

Every visual should feel suitable for a luxury travel campaign, architectural exhibition, or premium documentary.

### DO NOT USE

Do not use:

* SVG illustrations
* Flat vector artwork
* Cartoon-style graphics
* Animated illustrations
* Anime-style visuals
* Comic-style artwork
* Generic AI-generated artwork
* Low-resolution images
* Pixelated textures
* Placeholder illustrations
* Clipart
* Simplified icons as hero visuals

The website should never resemble an animated website or game interface.

All scenes should feel authentic, realistic, and premium.

## REALISTIC SAKURA SYSTEM

Replace all current gamified sakura petal effects.

Do not use:

* Floating particle systems
* Cartoon petals
* Repetitive animations
* Artificial particle emitters
* Excessive visual clutter

Instead create:

* Realistic cherry blossom petals
* Natural wind simulation
* Organic falling motion
* Different sizes
* Multiple depth layers
* Foreground and background petals
* Cinematic movement
* Natural rotation while falling
* Soft depth-of-field blur

The petals should appear as if they are being carried naturally by the wind.

The effect should feel subtle, cinematic, and realistic.

Use restraint.

Less is more.
